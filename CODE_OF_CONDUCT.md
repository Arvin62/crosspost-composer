# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

This project welcomes people who report reproducible problems, document platform
behavior, improve accessibility, and help other users. Participation must remain
professional, specific, and respectful.

本项目欢迎可复现的问题报告、平台兼容性记录、无障碍改进建议以及对其他用户的帮助。
参与讨论时请保持专业、具体和尊重。

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the implementation or observed behavior, not a person's identity.
- Provide synthetic examples instead of unpublished articles or personal data.
- Respect coordinated security disclosure and licensing boundaries.
- State conflicts of interest, commercial relationships, or automated content
  when they materially affect a report or recommendation.
- Accept that platform behavior may differ by account, browser, region, and date;
  record those conditions instead of dismissing another person's result.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private drafts
or credentials, knowingly false reports, fabricated adoption signals, and attempts
to pressure maintainers or contributors into undisclosed commercial arrangements
are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Public conduct concerns may be reported to the maintainer through a GitHub Issue
that does not repeat harmful or private material. Security or privacy incidents
must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer may edit
or remove content, lock a discussion, or restrict participation when necessary,
and will document public moderation actions where doing so does not amplify harm.
